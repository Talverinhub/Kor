# TalverinConnect and Kor Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to the Kor desktop app and the TalverinConnect browser extension, published by Talverin.

## What information is processed

TalverinConnect reads the song title, artist, cover link, playback state, duration and volume on YouTube Music.

The extension also keeps the connection status, language, site preference and current song in the browser's local storage. The current song is kept for the browser session.

Kor stores the following on your computer:

- Settings and routines
- Your notes
- Focus times, topics and the names of songs you listened to
- The state of a running timer
- A local log of unexpected errors
- If you connected Spotify, an encrypted Spotify login key tied to this computer
- The Spotify Client ID, if you entered one

## Where the information goes

The extension sends this information only to the Kor app on the same computer. It isn't sent to Talverin or third parties, it isn't sold, and it isn't used for advertising.

The extension doesn't read passwords, cookies or account information.

## External connections

The services below can see technical information, like your IP address, when they are contacted:

- If you connect your Spotify account, the app talks to Spotify directly. Spotify's own terms and privacy policy apply to this use.
- The app connects to GitHub to check for and download new versions.
- Song covers load from the music service's own address.

## What Kor does on your Spotify account

If you connect Spotify, Kor can read what's playing, control playback, add or remove liked songs and create a playlist. It changes your account only when you ask it to, for example by pressing the like button or saving the month's playlist.

You can remove Kor's access at any time on the [Spotify apps page](https://www.spotify.com/account/apps/).

## Audio

While rhythm reaction is on, the app processes the sound of the selected music source in real time. Audio isn't recorded and doesn't leave your computer.

## How long data is kept

Local data stays on your computer until you delete it. The five newest local settings backups made before updates are kept.

## Deleting your data

You can delete local data with Delete my data in Kor under Settings, General. Removing the extension from your browser also deletes its settings.

## Your rights

Talverin doesn't hold your data, because it stays on your computer. For questions or requests about your data, including under KVKK or GDPR, use the address below.
